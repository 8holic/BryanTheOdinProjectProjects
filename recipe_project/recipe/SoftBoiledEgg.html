Soft Boiled Egg

Description

]

Singaporean favorite simple breakfast, usually served with pepper

Ingredients

  • 2 egg
  • Boiling Water
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Boil some water using a kettle
  2. Place the two eggs inside a pot submerged in boiling water,close the lid
  3. Wait for around 6 minute 30 seconds
  4. Remove egg from shell and crack the egg with a spoon
  5. Serve hot immediately with some pepper
