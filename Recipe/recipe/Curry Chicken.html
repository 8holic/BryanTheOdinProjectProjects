Curry Chicken

Description

]

A curry chicken dish passed down from my grandmother

This dish has a coconut flavour, suitable for around 3-4 people

Ingredients

    1 Whole Chicken 1 teaspoon of curry leaves 100ml of coconut milk 3 Red Chili Padi 1kg potatos skinned 1 teaspoon of salt one A1 curry stock

Steps

Put the curry stock, the curry leave, and the chicken in the pot Put the skinned potato into salted water Fry the mixture until the paste turn into a dark yellow Pour 750ml of water into the pot Keep the mixture at near boiling point,add the salt Wait for around 30 minutes Pour the coconut milk and cook at low heat for around 2 minutes The dish is now ready to be served
