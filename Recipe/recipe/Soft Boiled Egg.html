Soft Boiled Egg

Description

]

Singaporean favorite simple breakfast, usually served with pepper

Ingredients

    2 egg Boiling Water Pepper

Steps

    Boil some water using a kettle Place the two eggs inside a pot submerged in boiling water,close the lid Wait for around 6 minute 30 seconds Remove egg from shell and crack the egg with a spoon Serve hot immediately with some pepper
